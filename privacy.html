Privacy Policy

GetQuickHaul — Community Cargo Delivery

Effective date: October 27, 2026

GetQuickHaul ("we," "us") operates the cargo van pickup and delivery service at GetQuickHaulNJ.com and the GetQuickHaul mobile app. This policy explains what information we collect when you use our website or app, and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you request a quote or book a delivery, we collect the information you give us:

  • Your name, phone number, and email address
  • Pickup and delivery addresses
  • Trip details, such as dates, times, and notes about the items to be delivered
  • If you register as a driver, the contact and vehicle details you provide on the driver form

We do not collect your precise device location. Addresses are the ones you type in for your booking.

How we use your information

  • To provide quotes, schedule and complete your pickup and delivery
  • To send your booking confirmation and to alert our driver/dispatch about new bookings
  • To contact you about your booking by phone, text, or email
  • To keep ordinary business records, such as invoices and receipts

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not share your information with advertisers, and we do not use it for third-party marketing.
  • We do not use advertising trackers in our app.

SMS text messaging

If you book a delivery with us, or register with us as a driver, you agree to receive text messages about your booking or dispatch — such as booking confirmations and delivery alerts — at the mobile number you provide. Message frequency varies with your bookings. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message; reply HELP for help. Message and data rates may apply.

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Service providers

We use a small number of service providers to run the service — for example, email delivery for booking confirmations and map/open-street data to display addresses and calculate mileage. These providers receive only the information needed to perform their function.

Payments

Payments are handled outside the app (for example Cash App, cash, check, or card by phone). We do not store full card numbers in the app or on the website.

Keeping and deleting information

We keep booking records for as long as needed for business and tax purposes. To ask us to update or delete your information, contact us at getquickhaul@gmail.com or (551) 233-8583, and we will take care of it.

Children

Our service is for adults arranging deliveries. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will post the new version on this page with a new effective date.
