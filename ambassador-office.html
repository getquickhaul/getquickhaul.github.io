Why you get paid

GetQuickHaul grows the way neighborhoods actually work: one trusted person telling another. That person is you. When someone books with your personal code, that booking is credited to you — and you earn 5% of the fare.

How the money works, plainly:

· Your 5% comes out of the platform's 20% share. The driver's 80% is never touched — that's a promise the company runs on.

· Example: a $54.30 booking made with your code puts $2.72 in your balance. Ten bookings like that is about $27.

· Balances are paid monthly, once your balance reaches $25.

· The office keeps your ledger — every booking credited to your code — and sends you a monthly earnings report by text or email, so you can check every dollar yourself.

How to talk about GetQuickHaul

Say this: Community Cargo Delivery — local pickup and delivery by neighbors, from $45. Book in a minute at GetQuickHaulNJ.com or in the app. Drivers are local van owners who keep 80% of every fare — it's a platform, not a job: they build their own business on it.

Never promise: an exact price for someone's trip (their booking gives them their exact price), a pickup time the office hasn't confirmed, or how much a driver will earn. If you're not sure, send them to the office — that's what we're here for.
