Driver Office — Start Here

Welcome to the platform. GetQuickHaul is not a job and you're not an employee — it's a vehicle you get in to build your own business: we bring the platform, the bookings and the customers; you bring the van and the professionalism. You keep 80% of every fare.

This page is your first training. Before your first trip, know these standards cold — they're the agreement every driver runs under.

1. Dress professional

You are the face the customer sees — the whole company, in their doorway. Arrive clean and professional: clean clothes, closed shoes, no torn or dirty workwear. When your GetQuickHaul gear arrives, wear it. Keep the van clean inside and out; a clean van is a five-star review before you say a word.

2. Be courteous — every stop, every time

· Greet the customer by name. Text or call when you're on your way and when you arrive.
· Be patient — with elders, with busy store staff, with a customer who's mid-move and stressed.
· Never argue with a customer. If something goes wrong — damage, a delay, a disagreement — stay calm and call the office: (551) 233-8583. That's what the office is for.
· Thank them at the door. Courtesy is why customers book again — and ask for you.

3. Check the merchandise

· Before you load: walk the items with the customer. Count them. Look at the condition together — if something is already scratched or broken, say it out loud and note it before you touch it. A photo of the load protects you and the customer.
· Load it right: secure everything so nothing shifts, slides or crushes on the road.
· At drop-off: check the items off together before you leave. Cash, check and COD are collected before unloading — that's the house rule, no exceptions.

4. The agreement — what we carry, and what we NEVER carry

We carry: furniture, boxes, store merchandise, appliances (curb to curb), business freight and route deliveries — the honest cargo of a neighborhood.

NEVER — no exceptions, no side deals:

· No firearms or ammunition.
· No drugs or powders of any kind — nothing illegal, nothing that "looks like" it.
· Nothing stolen, nothing unlawful, no hazardous chemicals or fuel.

If a customer asks you to carry anything on this list, refuse politely — "I'm sorry, our agreement doesn't allow me to carry that" — and call the office. A driver who takes one of these loads, on or off the platform, is off the platform. This rule protects your license, your van, and your freedom. It is not negotiable.

5. Run it like a business

· On time is the standard — if you're running late, the customer hears it from you first, not from the clock.
· Phone charged, app open, trip details read before you roll.
· No passengers on a booked trip, and never a trip "off the books" for a platform customer — that's how drivers lose the platform.
· Your earnings, your trips and your ratings live in the app and your driver record — the office can walk you through them any time.

Questions before your first trip? Call or text the office at (551) 233-8583 or email getquickhaul@gmail.com. Not a driver yet? Join us at Driver Launch Night.
