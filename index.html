GetQuickHaul is a small, local delivery service based in Weehawken, New Jersey. We help neighbors and local businesses move packages and small loads by cargo van — and drivers like you are the heart of it.

Good to know.

What kinds of jobs do you handle?

GetQuickHaul handles packages, boxes and small loads that fit safely in a cargo van.

Do you handle full-house moves?

No. The service is focused on smaller pickup and delivery jobs rather than large moves.

How is my price calculated?

The door-to-door quote is $45 for the first 5 miles, then $3 for each additional mile. Extra stops are $15 each. A $25 NYC toll surcharge applies when the route crosses the Hudson to or from Manhattan. Pickups before 7 AM or after 7 PM have a $15 after-hours surge.

How can I pay?

You can pay with Cash App ($GetQuickHaul), in cash, cash on delivery (COD), or by check made payable to GetQuickHaul. If you’d rather arrange payment another way, choose manual payment when you book and GetQuickHaul will set it up with you. Nothing is due until your booking is confirmed.

What items cannot be transported?

GetQuickHaul will not carry appliances; illegal or stolen goods; controlled substances; firearms or ammunition; hazardous, flammable or explosive materials; or anything unsafe to transport. We may inspect or refuse a load.

Can I enter an address outside New Jersey?

Yes. Address search covers all 50 states. Bookings are open to pickups in our two service areas — Weehawken, North Jersey and the NYC metro area, and South Florida: Hollywood and Broward County, plus South Dade (Cutler Bay and Palmetto Bay) — the drop-off can be anywhere, and the price is based on the driving miles. If the pickup or an extra stop is outside those areas, you’ll see “No drivers available in this area temporarily” and the booking will not continue.

About GetQuickHaul

GetQuickHaul — Pickup & Delivery Service in Weehawken, NJ

GetQuickHaul provides fast, careful pickup and delivery for packages, small loads, furniture, and store or warehouse pickups across Weehawken, Hudson County, North Jersey, and the New York City metro area — and now South Florida: Hollywood and Broward County, plus South Dade (Cutler Bay and Palmetto Bay). Book online in minutes and get an instant price before you confirm: $45 covers the first 5 miles, then $3 per mile after that, door to door. Extra stops are $15 each, and deliveries to New York City include a $25 toll surcharge. We are open 7 AM to 7 PM every day; pickups outside those hours carry a $15 after-hours charge for new customers.

What we deliver

  • Store, warehouse & marketplace pickups
  • Business deliveries on a repeat schedule
  • Driver opportunities — owner-operators earn 80% of every fare
