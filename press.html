GetQuickHaul to Hudson County: Freedom Is a Gift — Protect It With Your Vote

Local Community Cargo Delivery company launches its press page with a civic message as early voting continues in New Jersey.

GetQuickHaul, the Community Cargo Delivery service based in Weehawken, today published the first release on its new press page — and it isn't about deliveries. It's about voting.

The company's message, shared with a photo of local voters in line, reads:

"Freedom is a gift. Prosperity is a blessing. And neither one is guaranteed forever.

Nations that took it for granted learned the hard way. Our vote is how we protect what we have."

GetQuickHaul is a one-van, locally run delivery service built on a simple idea — neighbors helping neighbors. The company says that same spirit is what Election Day is about: ordinary people showing up for their community and their country.

"We carry boxes for a living," the company said. "But the heaviest thing any of us carries is our vote. This is us asking our neighbors: use it, and use it with your eyes open."

About GetQuickHaul

GetQuickHaul — Community Cargo Delivery — is a local cargo van pickup and delivery service based in Weehawken, New Jersey, serving Weehawken, Union City, West New York, Jersey City, Hoboken, North Bergen, Guttenberg, and Secaucus, with pickups across the New York metro and North Jersey. Services include store pickups (Costco, IKEA, Home Depot), packages, and small loads for neighbors and local businesses, with local trips from $45 and online booking at GetQuickHaulNJ.com.
